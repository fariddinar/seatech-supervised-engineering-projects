## AI & Chatbot Projects

### Les Chats de SeaTech (Hugging Face Deployment) – Mohamed Ali SHAIEK & Anthony HERVE

* **Description**: Deployment of a SeaTech chatbot on Hugging Face based on a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture, providing personalized and context-aware responses.  
* **Deployment**: https://huggingface.co/spaces/SeatechProjet/Les_Chats_De_SeaTech
